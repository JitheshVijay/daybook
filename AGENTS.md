# Prototype Instructions

## User-approved scope and design

- Build a functional personal app first; defer public accounts, billing, and social features.
- Phone-friendly web app, not a native app or a device-frame prototype.
- Tracked activities: workouts (sets, reps, weight, muscle heatmap), reading with books and covers, food with calories and macros, guitar practice with songs, meditation with quality, dreams, steps, cardio, screen time, sleep, and custom habits. Use real persistent empty-state records, never seeded personal history.
- **2026-09-13, chat-first redesign (current direction).** The user asked for one ChatGPT/Claude-style conversation instead of "Log my day" and "Ask assistant", graphs "like Mixpanel" for each feature, calories taken from what they say they ate, follow-up questions when details are missing, and a smart assistant.
  - Structure: **Chat** (home) · **Insights** (per-feature charts) · **Journal** (calendar and every entry, edit/delete) · **Settings**. The old per-feature pages were folded into Insights tabs by the user's choice. Manual logging stays available from the header and sidebar.
  - Visual direction: **shadcn/ui**. The user rejected three custom directions with "make it like shadcn". Use real shadcn components (style radix-nova, neutral theme, Tailwind v4, lucide icons, Geist), light/dark following the system, shadcn `sidebar` (expanded ≥1024px, icon rail 768–1023px, sheet on phones) and shadcn `chart` (Recharts). The charcoal/lime mock (`~/Downloads/daybook-option-2.png`) and the five-entry bottom nav are superseded.
  - Replies arrive whole (no token streaming). Food without numbers is estimated automatically and labelled as an estimate.
  - Chart series colours use the validated categorical palette in `src/index.css` (`--chart-1…5`); check any new colour with the dataviz validator.
- 2026-09-13: the AI runs on OpenRouter through `server/provider.mjs` (`OPENROUTER_*` preferred, `OPENAI_*` and `OPENAI_BASE_URL` still work). Keep both paths and the simulated-provider tests.
- Local persistence and export are present; cross-device sync is not.

## How the assistant works (keep these guarantees)

- `POST /api/chat` is a stateless proxy: it adds the system prompt (`server/chat-prompt.mjs`) and tool definitions (`src/chat-schema.js`) and returns the model's message. The browser executes tools against the in-memory records (`src/chat-tools.js`), which `src/store.js` saves to the local database and loops (`src/chat-loop.js`, at most 4 calls per turn).
- The app, not the model, decides what is complete (`prepareImport`/`draftIssues`). Incomplete items become pending questions; only complete entries are saved; every saved batch has Undo.
- Chart and number answers come from `src/insights.js`, never from the model.
- Repeated activities within one turn are saved once. Keep that protection; the model sometimes splits or repeats tool calls.
- 2026-09-13 (user report: a gym session came back as eight "Fill in" rows). Workouts never require a duration. One gym session is one workout, and one meal is one food entry, whatever shape the model sends. Complete exercises and foods save immediately; only the missing ones wait, grouped into one question per workout or meal, and answers join the saved entry. Several log calls in one reply run as one log. A forced retry accepts only the types it asked for. Re-sent sessions join the saved entry without overwriting saved values. Past-tense "planned" entries become done. Updates merge exercises and foods by name. "Fill in" is a secondary icon; the chat question is the primary path.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "make it fetch the calories because i dont know or count my calories"). Food nutrition is never asked of the user. After a log, foods without calories go to `POST /api/nutrition` (`server/nutrition.mjs`), which returns totals for what was eaten; the app divides by servings and saves the items as estimates in the same meal and card before the model replies. Only if that call fails does the food stay waiting. Waiting foods are estimated again on load. The manual food form has "Estimate calories".
- 2026-09-13 (user report: typed "Hip Adduction … - reps" without the number; the model saved 12 and never asked). Reps and weights for an exercise the app is waiting on must appear in the clause of the latest message that names it, or already be known; otherwise they are cleared, the exercise stays waiting, and the result lists it in `notInUserMessage` (`groundExercises`/`numbersForPart` in `src/chat-tools.js`). Other workout numbers must appear somewhere in the recent user messages. update_entry follows the same rule. Bump `RULES_VERSION` when completeness rules change so old waiting items are re-checked; re-checked gaps of a saved workout or meal re-join that entry.
- 2026-09-13 (user: calories too low, "should be more than that"). `/api/nutrition` asks for grams eaten and per-100 g values and the server multiplies, with home-portion weight guidance (big plate of rice ≈ 450 g, whole fried fish ≈ 150 g). A whole-meal or whole-workout correction is update_entry with `replaceList: true`; foods in a log (including the model's own guesses) or a correction are estimated by the app before the call runs (`prefillNutrition`); user-given numbers are kept.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "it still shows this after a refresh"). Meals saved with older estimates are re-estimated once per browser on load (`reestimateMeals`, flag `daybook.repair.nutrition.v2`), only for meals shown on a card in the chat, using the message that logged them. A change of 10% or more appears as an update card with Undo.
- 2026-09-13 (user: guitar songs "with a fetched song cover like we do for books", "what all songs i should practice", "use a retention algorithm … scientifically"). Songs get album art from the iTunes Search API (`src/song-covers.js`, confident title+artist matches only). Chat tools `add_songs` (want to practice, no session) and `practice_plan`. Guitar sessions carry a 1–4 rating in `quality` (Couldn't play it, Rough, Solid, Clean); the assistant asks with ask_user when it's missing. `src/practice.js` runs FSRS-5 with default parameters per song (unrated = Solid), schedules a review at 90% retention, and plans today: due songs by lowest retention, then one new song (two when nothing is due), one song per 5 goal minutes, minutes split by difficulty. Guitar tab: Practice today, Retention forecast, Practice list. Song status "Want to learn" was added.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "why is it not fetching properly", songs without covers). A title alone is ambiguous on iTunes (a search for "Neon" ranks another artist first), so a cover is only chosen once the artist is known. Songs without an artist go to `POST /api/songs` (`server/songs.mjs`) with the recordings iTunes found; the model picks the intended song (fixing typos such as "Aruvian Dance" → "Aruarian Dance", Nujabes) or marks an exercise as unknown. An artist the user gave is never replaced; if iTunes doesn't have it, the song's best-known art is shown. The cover retry on load is keyed by `COVER_LOOKUP_VERSION`.
- 2026-09-13 (user: leg press "should be 100kg right i kept adding weights to it"). Plate maths is done by the app, not the model: `statedWeights`/`applyStatedWeights` in `src/chat-tools.js` read each exercise's clauses ("1st set of 40 kg, 2nd i added 2 plates 15kg" → 40, 70; "added 2 plates 15kg" = two 15 kg plates; "each side" doubles; removals subtract) and fill a single stated weight ("with 60kg") into sets the model left empty. Exercise names in clauses tolerate typos ("deadlisfts", "extention") but never swap real exercise words (abduction/adduction). Corrections count saved values as known, so "the sets were 40, 70, 90 and 100 kg" keeps the reps; a copied summary in notes or a generic title in a correction is ignored.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "it calculated as if i did 6 sets now"). Guards against extra sets: a set count stated for an exercise ("leg press of 4 sets") drops exact repeated sets beyond it (`statedSetCount`, `dropRepeatedSets`); a correction can't add repeated sets beyond the saved count unless the user reports more sets; `replaceList` with only some of the saved exercises or foods is treated as a partial correction (it once removed every other exercise); reps or weights a correction changes without the user saying so keep their saved value (positional). Exercise history shows sets per session.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "why cant it calculate duration?" for "slept at 3 am … woke up at 8:40 am … nap at 3:30 pm then woke up at 6:40"). Entries have an `endTime`; the model extracts start and end times (resolving am/pm from context) and the app computes minutes (`minutesBetween` in `src/day-import.js`, wrapping past midnight) for sleep, meditation, guitar, cardio, workout, reading and screen time, replacing any minutes the model wrote.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "we need a heatmap for activity also. based on if we have put all entry"). The Journal has a GitHub-style logging heatmap (`src/components/journal/logging-heatmap.jsx`, `src/logging.js`). Each day's level is the share of daily trackers logged (0 nothing, 1 under a third, 2 a third or more, 3 two thirds or more, 4 full day); planned entries don't count. The daily trackers are `profile.dailyTrackers` when set (the card's "Full day" popover), otherwise the daily kinds the user logged in the last 30 days (food, sleep, steps, screen time, meditation, habit), otherwise food and sleep. Colors are the `--heat-0…4` tokens: one blue ramp, brighter for fuller days in dark mode. The summary shows full days, the streak (to yesterday while today is open) and days with entries.
- 2026-09-13 (user asked for a night teeth-brushing tracker): teeth brushing is a habit, not a type. `add_habits` sets up a habit without logging. A yes-or-no habit is unit "times" with goal 1, which is also the default for habits first created from a log. The Journal checks these off in one tap, with Undo. The prompt matches habits by meaning and respects a time of day in the name.
- 2026-09-14 (user: "remove \"work\" i cant really measure or log it"). The Work tracker was removed from the chat tools, prompt, entry form, Insights and context. "work" is in `RETIRED_TYPES` so any old work entries (and a "work" daily tracker) still load and can be deleted from the Journal. Don't re-add a work tracker unless the user asks.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "there is an issue with books": "till 51 pages and finished 11 chapters" became 17/92 chapters, and "11/92 not 17/92" changed nothing but claimed an update). Reading entries have position fields `fromPage`, `toPage` and `chaptersFinished`. `readingFromPosition` turns them into session amounts from the book's progress, excluding the session being corrected, and moves the book's `startPages`/`startChapters` so progress lands exactly where the user said. The `update_book` tool corrects position (totals so far), length, status or author. Book changes are kept in `card.replacedBooks`, which Undo restores. A correction that changes nothing returns `nothingChanged` and no card. context.books includes pagesRead/totalPages/chaptersRead/totalChapters.
- 2026-09-14 (user: "instead of browser storage, add a proper database"). Records and chat live in SQLite (`server/database.mjs`, better-sqlite3, `data/daybook.db` or `DAYBOOK_DB`), served by `server/data-api.mjs` at `/api/data`. The API is loopback-only because the dev server listens on 0.0.0.0 with no login, and writes need a same-origin JSON request. The client (`src/store.js`) keeps records in memory and saves each commit as a diff (`src/sync.js`), queuing unsaved changes in localStorage (`daybook.unsavedChanges`) with retries. It reloads when the database version moves (another tab or browser). On first run it moves localStorage data into an empty database (`daybook.movedToDatabase`). Browser storage stays as the fallback (other devices, Sites build, database unavailable). The server re-validates every save with `validateState`. **Browser tests must import `test` from `tests/browser/fixtures.mjs`**, which blocks `/api/data`: the suite runs against the user's live dev server and must never touch their database.
- 2026-09-14 (user: "see this": after an Undo, the same move request made the model try undo_batch and claim the egg was already moved). `runTurn` now handles a clear move request itself, before any model call. When `moveRequest` finds from/to days and exactly one done entry on the from day matches the user's words, the app runs update_entry (date only) and replies "Moved … from … to …". It writes a well-formed tool exchange into history. Ambiguous requests still go to the model.
- 2026-09-14 (user: "it said it moved but its still showing the egg in 12th september"). The model updated the 13 September dinner (adding an egg, renaming it "Food") instead of moving the 12 September egg. The app now parses move requests ("move X from <day> to <day>": `moveRequest`/`dayFromPhrase`). update_entry refuses entries that aren't on the "from" day and names the right id in the hint. A move changes only the date. A move sent as log_entries moves the matching entry on the "from" day instead. Generic titles ("Food", the type label, "Meal") never replace a name. Change lists only show fields the entry type uses.
- 2026-09-14 (user: "what's happening?": "move the fried egg from September 12th to September 13th" deleted their Sleep entry; live replays also deleted a whole lunch). The app itself guards deletes. `delete_entries` only runs when the latest message asks to delete or remove something (`DELETE_INTENT`) and each entry matches the user's words (`namesEntry`) or was logged in the last few turns (`recentLoggedIds`). `undo_batch` needs undo wording. Moving is update_entry on the entry's id with a new date. context.recentEntries lists entries from the last 3 days with ids and item names, so the model can find them. Date-correction wording ("meaning the 13th not the 12th", "I meant", "wrong day") moves a recently logged entry instead of logging it again (`CORRECTION_CUE`). "One more"/"another" never merges into a same-day meal as a duplicate (`MORE_OF`).
- 2026-09-14 (user: "i meant 13th but it saved for 12th": with the composer set to Yesterday, "I had one more fried egg yesterday" went to the day before). Relative days ("yesterday", "today", "last night", weekdays) count from the real date with the 4 am rule, never from the composer's logDate, which is only the default when no day is named. `statedDay` in `src/chat-tools.js` checks each entry's source (then the latest message) and corrects the model's date. Explicit dates, "tomorrow", and messages naming several different days are left to the model. Logged cards show the date the entries were saved on.
- 2026-09-14 (user: "fix this": a meditation waiting for its rating wasn't saved when the reply gave the rating and a corrected date). Waiting items can be completed however the model phrases it: `update_entry` on a pending id completes it through log_entries with resolves; a log of the same activity (same type, status and name, any date) clears the one waiting; a pending item completed earlier in the same reply is never saved again (`turnResolved` in `src/chat-loop.js`). The chat's default logging day runs until 4 am (`loggingDay` in `src/domain.js`): after midnight, "today" is the day that just ended, and the date chip shows "Yesterday (late night)". A picked date resets when the calendar day changes.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "can i add wishlist to read for books also?"). Chat tool `add_books` (reading list, status "Want to read", no session); `add_songs` is music only (before this, the model put books into the song list). Book titles match loosely (`bookKey`: punctuation, leading article, subtitle) for sessions and covers; covers fill a missing author. A first done session moves a book from "Want to read" to "Reading" and a song from "Want to learn" to "Learning". The Reading tab lists reading first, then want to read.
- 2026-09-13 (user: "adductors are not highlighted"). For known exercise names the app's muscle (`inferMuscle`, whole-word rules) wins over the model's pick; assistant-made workouts are repaired once per browser (`repairAssistantMuscles`, flag `daybook.repair.muscles.v1`). react-body-highlighter mislabels thigh areas (front inner thigh is "abductors", back inner thigh is "adductor", no outer hip): the muscle map draws adductor sets on both inner-thigh areas and hip abductor sets on the glutes (`BODY_AREAS` in `training-tab.jsx`).
- 2026-09-15 (user: "why cant it fetch?": "250 gm broasted chicken, 1 kuboos, 1 porotta and 50 gm hummus" left every food asking for calories). The model had used grams as servings (250, 50), and `/api/nutrition` rejected the whole request because servings were over 100. Weights and volumes are amounts, not counts: `weightsAsAmounts` in `src/chat-tools.js` turns a food whose servings number sits next to a unit and the food's name in the user's words into servings 1 named "broasted chicken, 250 g", clearing guessed numbers but keeping the user's own. It runs in `prefillNutrition`, in `logEntries`, and in `fillFoodNutrition` (on reload using the message that left the meal waiting). The chat prompt and the estimator prompt say the same. The nutrition endpoint never rejects over an amount: servings that aren't a 1–100 count are ignored, and the server settles the count itself.
- 2026-09-15 (user: "what happened to my back workout yesterday"). Chat logs could silently never reach the database. The save queue in `src/store.js` removed "the first item" after each request, but a conversation save replaces older conversation saves in the queue while one is on its way, which can move a record change to the front; that change was then dropped. It hit entries saved within 300 ms of the assistant's tool-call message (workouts, foods with the user's own calories); estimated foods survived because the lookup took seconds. Finished requests now remove exactly the item they sent. Lost entries were rebuilt by replaying the recorded tool calls through `executeToolCall` with the original turn ids (same entry ids and card summaries) and written through `/api/data/changes`. Browser test: a slow chat save while a workout and a food are logged.
